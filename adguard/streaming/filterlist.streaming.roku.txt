! ==========================================
! REGEX — variant catching (AdGuard Home / AdGuard DNS only)
! Added 2026-09-02. Source: ozankiratli gist (updated 2026-09-02) + r/pihole 1v7jmeq.
! Exact hostnames above miss prefixed/suffixed and deeper-nested variants:
! amoeba2.roku.com, giga.sb.roku.com, foo.austin.roku.com etc.
! If Roku breaks, delete THIS BLOCK FIRST — the exact hosts above are the safe base.
! 'lb' is deliberately excluded from the name list: as a substring it matches far
! too much. lb.roku.com is covered by the exact rule above.
! ==========================================

! Ad/telemetry labels at any depth.
! 'cti' deliberately excluded: it would catch ls.cti/tis.cti.roku.com, which are
! whitelisted above for Roku Channel playback. ||cti.roku.com^ above covers the base.
/^(([^.]+\.)*(logs|ads|web|prod\.mobile|voice)(\.[^.]+)*\.)roku\.com$/

! Rotating codename hosts, with characters before/after and deeper subdomains
/^(([^.]+\.)*[^.]*(amarillo|amoeba|austin|benjamin|bryan|camden|cooper|copper|digdug|external|giga|gilbert|griffin|hereford|liberty|littlefield|longview|madison|marlin|midland|paolo|richmond|rollingwood|scribe|sugarland|tyler|victoria|windsor)[^.]*(\.[^.]+)*\.)roku\.com$/

! Prefix hosts (adds customer-feedbacks, seen in r/pihole Aug 2026)
/^((captive|cloudservices|wwwimg|customer-feedbacks)\.)roku\.com$/

! Tracking under *.sr.roku.com (whitelisted content hosts above are unaffected)
/^((bif|microsites|traces|track|userdata)\.sr\.)roku\.com$/

! Roku ad measurement. The community regex misses roku.eu.admeasurement.com; the apex
! is an ad-measurement domain only, so blocking it covers every arrangement.
||admeasurement.com^
