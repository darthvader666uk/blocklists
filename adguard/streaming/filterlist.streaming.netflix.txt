! ==========================================
! READ THIS FIRST — WHY THIS LIST IS SMALL
! ==========================================
! Netflix serves ad-tier adverts via SSAI (server-side ad insertion): the ad
! segments are stitched into the same encrypted HLS/DASH stream, from the same
! first-party domains, as the programme itself. There is no separate ad request
! to block. Blocking the delivery domain blocks the video too.
!
! DNS-level ad removal on Netflix is therefore NOT POSSIBLE — not with AdGuard
! Home, not with Pi-hole, not with a browser extension. Anything claiming
! otherwise is blocking third-party measurement, not the ads.
!
! The only reliable fix is the subscription tier. On Sky (Glass/Stream), Netflix
! is bundled at "Standard with Ads" by DEFAULT on both Essential and Ultimate TV,
! and Sky's Ad Skipping pack explicitly does NOT cover Netflix. Ad-free is a paid
! upgrade through Sky: +£6/mth (Standard) or +£11/mth (Premium 4K).
!
! Sep 2026 context — expect ad load to keep rising:
! - Netflix nearly DOUBLED US upfront ad commitments (announced 10 Aug 2026).
! - Netflix confirmed (May 2026) it is testing PERSONALISED ad loads and
!   frequency caps, so load varies per household and changes without notice.
!   A sudden increase on one night is consistent with this, not with a filter
!   regression.
!
! What this list does: blocks ad measurement / attribution / verification
! partners. It reduces tracking. It does not reduce the number of adverts.
! Effectiveness against adverts: ~0%. Against ad telemetry: good.
!
